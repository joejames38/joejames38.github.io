The Growing Dice System

Arcana College uses a simple system of growing dice. Instead of traditional character statistics, every character has a collection of skills.

Every skill begins at d4. Whenever you roll the maximum value on your skill die, that skill grows to the next die.

d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → d20

A student who repeatedly practices a skill will naturally become better at it. Your character's abilities grow through the things they actually do during play.

How Skills Grow

1

Choose a Skill

When an action calls for a skill, roll that skill's die.

2

Roll the Die

Roll the die associated with the skill you are using.

3

Hit the Maximum

If you roll the highest possible number, the skill grows.

4

Grow!

Upgrade the skill to the next die in the progression.

Example: Potions

Mira begins the year with Potions d4. During an experiment, she rolls a 4.

Potions d4 → Potions d6

Later, she rolls a 6 while brewing a particularly difficult potion. Her Potions skill grows again.

Potions d6 → Potions d8

Mira becomes better at Potions simply by using it.

✦

Spellcasting

Casting, aiming, shaping, and manipulating magical spells.

◇

Transfiguration

Changing the form or properties of objects and creatures.

☀

Charms

Creating magical effects that influence objects, creatures, and the environment.

✧

Enchanting

Embedding persistent magical effects into objects, locations, or people.

⚗

Potions

Brewing, identifying, modifying, and using magical concoctions.

☿

Alchemy

♧

Herbology

♞

Magical Creatures

Understanding, handling, communicating with, and surviving magical creatures.

⚔

✈

☾

📜

♛

🗝

Learn By Doing

There are no traditional ability scores or piles of modifiers. Your character's skills tell the story of what they have learned.

Practice Potions and you become a better potion-maker. Spend your evenings flying and your Flying die grows. Get into trouble and your Cunning may become legendary.

"Your character sheet is a record of what you have survived."
