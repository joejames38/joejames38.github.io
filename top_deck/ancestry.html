Ancestry Rules
I

Ancestry

Your ancestry represents the people, culture, and natural talents you were born with. Ancestry provides specialized abilities without determining your class or core attributes.

Ancestry Does Not Modify Attributes

Ancestry never changes Might, Spirit, Dexterity, or Cunning. Your attributes are determined separately from ancestry.

During character creation, choose one ancestry. Then choose two ancestry feats from the four available to that ancestry.

II

Ancestry Choices

Each ancestry provides four ancestry feats. Choose two when creating your character.

III

Choosing Ancestry Feats

  1. Choose one ancestry.
  2. Review the four ancestry feats available to that ancestry.
  3. Choose two ancestry feats.
  4. Record the chosen feats on your character sheet.

Ancestry feats are permanent character features unless a later rule specifically allows a character to change them.
