Realm & Rune
Gear

Adventuring Gear

Adventurers rely on more than weapons and magic. Rope, lanterns, food, tools, clothing, and other equipment allow characters to survive the wilderness, explore dangerous ruins, and solve problems that cannot be handled with a sword or spell.

Equipment Philosophy

Realm & Rune uses relatively simple equipment rules. Gear should create interesting choices without requiring players to track dozens of minor statistics.

I

Currency

The standard currency of Realm & Rune is divided into four denominations. Each denomination is worth ten of the denomination immediately below it.

Copper Piece (cp) 1 cp
Silver Piece (sp) 10 cp
Gold Piece (gp) 100 cp
Platinum Piece (pp) 1,000 cp
Coin Value Common Uses
1 cp 1 copper Cheap food, small goods, minor services
1 sp 10 cp Meals, lodging, common equipment
1 gp 10 sp Weapons, armor, skilled services
1 pp 10 gp Major purchases, magical goods, valuable services
Currency Conversion

10 cp = 1 sp
10 sp = 1 gp
10 gp = 1 pp

Therefore: 1 pp = 100 gp = 1,000 sp = 10,000 cp

Weapon Suit Affinity

When a player plays a card matching their weapon's suit, the weapon may gain its associated bonus or mastery effect. Exact weapon mastery effects are defined by character abilities and feats.

III

Armor

Armor determines a character's base protection. Armor does not replace Dexterity or other defensive abilities unless its category specifically says so.

Defense Reminder

Base AC is: 5 + Dexterity when unarmored.

Light Armor: 5 + Armor Bonus + Dexterity

Medium Armor: 5 + Armor Bonus

Heavy Armor: 5 + Armor Bonus

Shields add +1 AC.

V

Food

Food is more than simple survival in Realm & Rune. Certain food and drink can be consumed through the Gobble action when a player burns an Ace.

Gobble

Burn an Ace to consume one food or drink item. Some items restore HP while others provide temporary benefits.

Only one persistent food effect may be active at a time. Consuming another food with a persistent effect removes the previous effect and replaces it.

Food Design Note

Food effects are intentionally modest. The Gobble mechanic turns Aces into a secondary resource that can compete with other reasons to hold cards without becoming a replacement for healing, magic, or class abilities.

VI

Equipment Rules

Detailed encumbrance rules are intentionally omitted from the current playtest rules. Characters are assumed to be able to carry a reasonable amount of adventuring equipment.

The GM may impose a limitation when a character is attempting to carry an obviously unreasonable amount of equipment.

Future Development

Future versions may add rules for ammunition, equipment durability, crafting, mounts, vehicles, magical equipment, and item rarity.
