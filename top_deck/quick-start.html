REALM & RUNE

QUICK START GUIDE • PLAYTEST EDITION

A lightweight heroic fantasy roleplaying game played with standard cards.

1. CHARACTER CREATION

  1. Choose an Ancestry. Choose two ancestry feats.
  2. Choose a Background. Gain its listed skill proficiencies and special ability.
  3. Choose a Class. Record your class features and starting proficiencies.
  4. Set Core Attributes. Might, Spirit, Dexterity, and Cunning range from -1 to +3.
  5. Choose equipment. Record weapons, armor, shield, and other equipment.
  6. Prepare your card deck. Each player uses their own 52-card deck.
Starting Hand: 2 cards.
Level 3: Hand Size 3.
Level 5: Hand Size 4 and +1 Core Attribute.

2. CORE ATTRIBUTES & SUITS

Suit Attribute Associated Concepts
♠ Spades Might Strength, endurance, melee combat
♥ Hearts Spirit Courage, leadership, healing, social actions
♣ Clubs Dexterity Agility, risk, precision
♦ Diamonds Cunning Knowledge, quick thinking, magic

Suits normally determine the type of ability being used. They do not automatically add an attribute modifier.

Add the appropriate Core Attribute modifier to attacks and skill checks.

3. THE CARD SYSTEM

Your deck is your action resource. Cards are drawn, played, burned, and recycled throughout the adventure.

Scene Start

At the beginning of a scene, draw cards until you reach your current Hand Size.

Start of Turn

Draw 1 card.

Playing Cards

You may play as many cards as you can afford during your turn. You may spend your entire hand.

At the end of your turn, discard down to your maximum Hand Size.

Action Economy:
1 Move Action + 1 Action for each card played.

You always draw at least one card each turn, guaranteeing that you can take an action.

4. CARD VALUES & PILES

Card Value / Function
2–9 Normal numerical value.
10 Value 10.
J / Q / K Value 10 or activate a special feature.
Ace Enables Gobble.

Shuffle Pile

Most played number cards and skill-check cards go here. When your deck is empty, shuffle the Shuffle Pile back into your deck.

Burn Pile

Face cards used for abilities and cards burned for magic are permanently removed from the current cycle.

A Short Rest shuffles all cards back into the deck.

5. SKILL CHECKS

When the GM calls for a skill check, use the skill's associated attribute and follow the appropriate proficiency rule.

Not Proficient

Draw 2 cards and use the lower value.

Proficient

Draw 1 card and play any one card from your hand. Resolve the check using the played card.

Expertise

Draw 1 card and play up to 2 cards. Add their values together, but the second card must match the suit affinity of the skill.

Add the relevant Core Attribute modifier.

Common Skills
Might: Athletics, Intimidation, Survival
Spirit: Insight, Persuasion, Deception, Religion/Occult, Performance
Dexterity: Acrobatics, Thievery, Animal Handling
Cunning: Perception, Arcana, Investigation, Medicine, History

6. COMBAT

Turn

On your turn, draw 1 card. You have 1 Move Action and may perform 1 Action per card played.

Might Strike

Play a card and make a Might attack with a weapon. On a hit, deal 1 HP damage. If the card matches the weapon's suit, deal 2 HP instead.

Ranged Strike

Play a card and make a Dexterity attack with a ranged weapon. On a hit, deal 1 HP damage. Matching the weapon's suit deals 2 HP.

Defense

Base AC = 5 + Dexterity when unarmored.

  • Light Armor: +1 AC + Dexterity
  • Medium Armor: +2 AC
  • Heavy Armor: +4 AC
  • Shield: +1 AC

Defend

Discard a card to add its value to your AC until the start of your next turn.

7. GENERAL ACTIONS & REACTIONS

Shield Bash — Action

Play a Spade while using a shield. Make an attack. On a hit, deal 1 HP damage and either push the target back or knock it prone.

If a face card is played, the target is both knocked back and knocked prone.

Rallying Cry — Action

Play a Heart. One ally may draw a Reserve Card.

If a face card is played, two allies may each draw a Reserve Card.

Dodge — Reaction

When hit, discard a Reserve Card to negate 1 HP of damage.

Gobble — Special Action

Burn an Ace to consume a food or drink item. Resolve the item's effect.

Only one persistent food effect may be active at a time. Consuming another persistent food replaces the previous effect.

8. MAGIC

Magic is divided into four resonant domains:

  • Arcane — ♦ Diamonds
  • Divine — ♥ Hearts
  • Primal — ♣ Clubs
  • Occult — ♠ Spades

Casting in Combat

Burn the required number of cards for the spell. The first burned card is added to the spell's to-hit check.

Each additional burned card that matches the spell's resonant suit provides +1 to the check.

Casting Outside Combat

Burn the required number of cards. You may search your deck for the cards you want to burn as long as they match the spell's resonant suit.

Spell Tiers

Tier Cost
0 Free outside combat; any card burned in combat.
1 1 card
2 2 cards
3 3 cards
4 4 cards
5 5 cards

9. SPELL RESONANCE & CHANNEL

Spell cards are burned rather than placed in the Shuffle Pile. Magic therefore consumes cards as a meaningful resource.

Resonant Suit

Every spell has a resonant suit. Additional cards matching that suit improve the spell's casting check.

Channel

Some spells have Channel. After paying the spell's normal casting cost, burn additional cards to increase the spell's effect.

Each additional resonant card used to Channel adds +1 damage or the listed Channel effect.

Example — Firebolt

Tier 1 Arcane spell. Burn 1 card to cast it. Make a Magic attack. On a hit, deal 1 HP damage.

Firebolt has Channel. After paying its normal cost, burn additional Diamonds to deal +1 HP damage per additional Diamond.

10. CLASSES

Fighter All weapons, armor, shields; 2 skills; Weapon Mastery; Second Wind. At Level 3, Action Surge.
Rogue Club weapons, Light Armor; 3 skills; Expertise; Backstab. At Level 3, Hide.
Cleric Heart weapons, Light/Medium Armor, Shields; 2 skills; Divine magic. Gains full access to Divine spell tiers.
Wizard 2 skills; learns individual Arcane spells and expands spell access as levels increase.
Level Cap: 5
Level 1 — Class Identity, Hand Size 2
Level 2 — Subclass Feature
Level 3 — Class Feature, Hand Size 3
Level 4 — Subclass Feature
Level 5 — +1 Attribute, Hand Size 4

11. FOOD, POTIONS & REST

Aces enable the Gobble action. Food and drink can restore health or provide temporary benefits.

Item Effect
Bread Recover 1 HP.
Health Potion Recover 3 HP.
Protein Bar +1 Might until the next Long Rest.
Fizzy Drink +1 Dexterity until the next Long Rest.

Persistent food effects do not stack. Consuming a new persistent food replaces the previous persistent effect.

Short Rest

Shuffle all cards back into your deck.

Long Rest

Recover HP and shuffle all cards back into your deck.

12. PLAYTEST MINDSET

Your Hand Is Your Toolbox.

You are not expected to save every good card. Spending cards creates more opportunities to act.

Powerful abilities may require you to burn valuable resources. Decide when the moment is worth it.

  • Ask what your character can accomplish with the cards you have.
  • Look for opportunities to match suits with weapons, abilities, and spells.
  • Do not be afraid to spend your entire hand.
  • Coordinate card-based abilities with your allies.
  • Tell the GM when you want to attempt something creative—the rules are intended to support heroic fantasy rather than restrict it.
