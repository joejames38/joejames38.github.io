One particularly important design question

The project has reached the point where I'd stop asking “What other mechanics should we add?” and start asking:

“What experience do we want a player to have during a typical 3-hour session?”

You already have enough mechanics to create a complete loop:

Explore → encounter → spend cards → fight → recover → gather food/resources → socialize/research → advance → face stronger enemies.

That loop is where the next round of playtesting should concentrate. The biggest opportunity now is probably cohesion and refinement, rather than adding another major subsystem.
