Rogue

Rogue

Agile specialists who rely on precision, deception, positioning, and opportunistic attacks.

Primary Attribute Dexterity
Resonant Suit Clubs
Armor Light Armor
Weapons Club Weapons
Role Striker / Specialist

Rogues are specialists in precision, mobility, deception, and exploiting weaknesses. They are particularly effective when they can choose the time and place of an engagement.

Rogue Identity

Rogues are not simply weaker fighters. Their strength comes from choosing advantageous situations, exploiting enemy weaknesses, and turning a small number of cards into highly effective actions.

I

Starting Proficiencies

  • Weapons: Club weapons
  • Armor: Light Armor
  • Skills: Choose any 3 skills
  • Expertise: Choose 1 proficient skill

Rogues excel at skills and gain more initial skill proficiencies than most classes.

II

Rogue Level Progression

Characters reach a maximum level of 5. Further development comes through Feat Points, equipment, and other campaign rewards.

III

Rogue Features

Backstab

Play a Club to make a Backstab attack. If the attack hits, deal 2 HP damage. If the card played is a face card, deal an additional 1 HP damage.

Backstab represents the Rogue's ability to exploit an opening rather than simply making a stronger version of a normal attack.

Hide

Discard a card to attempt to hide. Make an appropriate Dexterity-based check, normally using Stealth.

The GM determines whether the environment provides enough cover, concealment, darkness, or distraction to allow the attempt.

Rogue Expertise

When making a skill check in a skill in which you have Expertise, draw one card and play up to two cards from your hand.

If you play two cards, the second card must share the suit affinity of the skill. Add both card values together, then add the appropriate Core Attribute modifier.

IV

Assassin

Assassins specialize in choosing the right moment to strike. They use stealth, surprise, and preparation to deliver devastating opening attacks before an enemy can react.

Play Style: Stealth, ambushes, first-turn burst damage, target selection, and rapid elimination of dangerous enemies.

Assassin Features

Ambush

During the first round of combat, your first successful attack against a creature that has not yet acted deals +1 damage.

Deadly Opening

When you successfully attack a creature while hidden, you may burn a second Club to deal +2 damage.

Executioner

When attacking an enemy with 2 HP or less, deal +1 damage on a successful attack.

Vanish

After reducing an enemy to 0 HP, you may immediately discard a card to attempt to Hide.

Silent Step

You gain advantage on Stealth checks made to move without being detected.

Marked Target

At the beginning of combat, choose one enemy you can see. Your first successful attack against that enemy deals +1 damage.

V

Mastermind

Masterminds turn information into battlefield advantage. Rather than relying exclusively on personal damage, they coordinate allies, expose weaknesses, and manipulate enemies into making mistakes.

Play Style: Battlefield support, enemy debuffs, information, tactical coordination, and resource manipulation.

Mastermind Features

Analyze Target

Play a Diamond or Club and study an enemy. Until the beginning of your next turn, the next ally to attack that enemy gains +1 to their attack check.

Coordinated Strike

When an ally attacks an enemy you have analyzed, you may burn a card to allow that ally to draw one additional card for the attack.

Exploit Weakness

When you successfully identify an enemy's weakness, choose one ally. That ally deals +1 damage the next time they exploit that weakness.

Distracting Feint

Play a Club to distract an enemy. Until the beginning of your next turn, that enemy suffers -1 to its next attack against a target other than you.

Commanding Presence

When you use Rallying Cry, one affected ally may draw one additional card.

Calculated Retreat

When an ally within Near range is hit, you may burn a card to allow that ally to immediately move Close range without provoking an additional attack.

VI

Rogue Feats

These feats support continued development of Rogue characters after reaching level 5.

Improved Backstab

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Rogue

Your Backstab deals +1 additional damage when the attack card is a face card.

Shadow Step

Cost: 3 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Rogue, Stealth proficiency

After successfully hiding, you may move immediately without breaking Hide until you attack or take an obviously hostile action.

Opportunist

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Rogue

Once per round, when an enemy becomes vulnerable because of another character's action, you may draw one card.

Master of Disguise

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Rogue

Gain proficiency in Deception. When creating a mundane disguise, you may make a Deception check instead of relying on equipment or a disguise kit.

Tactical Assessment

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Mastermind

When you successfully analyze an enemy, you may also determine which of its known attacks deals the most damage.

Deadly Precision

Cost: 3 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Assassin

Once per scene, when you successfully hit an enemy from Hide, treat the attack card as a face card for determining additional damage.

VII

Playtest Notes

The Assassin and Mastermind intentionally approach the Rogue's core mechanics from opposite directions. The Assassin converts positioning and stealth into personal burst damage. The Mastermind converts information and cards into advantages for the entire party.

Assassin burst damage should be monitored carefully during playtesting because the card system already allows players to spend their entire hand in a single turn.

Mastermind abilities should be monitored for excessive card generation. Drawing additional cards is particularly valuable because cards represent both actions and future resource availability.
