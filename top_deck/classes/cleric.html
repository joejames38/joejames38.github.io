Cleric

Divine magic, conviction, healing, and the power to shape the battlefield through faith.

Primary Attribute Spirit
Resonant Suit Hearts
Armor Light, Medium, Shields
Weapons Heart Weapons
Spell Tradition Divine

Clerics channel divine power through Hearts. Their abilities emphasize healing, protection, leadership, conviction, and supernatural knowledge.

Cleric Identity

Clerics are versatile divine spellcasters who can support allies, protect the party, and confront supernatural threats.

A Cleric's choice of domain determines how their divine power manifests.

III

Cleric Features

Divine Spell Casting

Clerics use Hearts as their primary suit for Divine magic.

When casting a spell during combat, burn the required number of cards for the casting cost. The first card is added to the spell's magic attack check.

Clerics use Spirit as part of their magic attacks.

Cleric Spell save DC is 5 + Spirit.

Divine Spell Access

Clerics gain access to the complete Divine spell list appropriate to their level.

Tier 1 at level 1, Tier 2 at level 3, and Tier 3 at level 5.

Tier 4 and Tier 5 Spells are gained via Feats.

Channel Divinity

Burn a 2 and use an available Channel Divinity Feature.

Turn Undead

A cleric holds up their hold symbol and all undead in the near range must make Spirit Saving Throw against against the Cleric's spell save DC. On a save nothing happens, on a Fail all Undead immediately use their reaction to move to their maximum distance away and become frightened.

IV

Knowledge Domain

Clerics of the Knowledge Domain seek understanding above all else. Their divine gifts reveal secrets, expose weaknesses, and allow them to understand creatures, artifacts, and forgotten places.

Play Style: Investigation, information gathering, magical knowledge, and exploiting enemy weaknesses.

Knowledge Domain Features

Divine Scholar

Gain proficiency in Arcana, History, or Investigation.

Flash of Knowledge

While out of combat, you may burn a face-card of Diamonds to gain proficiency in any skill.

Only one instance of this feature may be active at any time.

V

War Domain

Clerics of the War Domain channel divine power through strength, discipline, and righteous battle. They stand among their allies rather than behind them.

Play Style: Front-line combat, weapon attacks, protection, battlefield leadership, and aggressive use of divine power.

War Domain Features

War Priest

Gain proficiency with one additional weapon set.

Divine Strike

Once per turn when you hit with a weapon, after the attack hits, you may burn an additional Heart to deal +1 damage.
