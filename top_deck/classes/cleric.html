Clerics channel divine power through Hearts. Their abilities emphasize healing, protection, leadership, conviction, and supernatural knowledge.

Cleric Identity

Clerics are versatile divine spellcasters who can support allies, protect the party, and confront supernatural threats.

A Cleric's choice of domain determines how their divine power manifests.

III

Cleric Features

Divine Spell Casting

Clerics use Hearts as their resonant suit for Divine magic.

When casting a spell during combat, burn the required number of cards. The first card is added to the spell's magic attack check.

Additional cards used to pay the casting cost do not normally contribute to the attack check. However, each additional card that matches the spell's resonant suit provides +1 to the check.

Spells with the Channel property may burn additional resonant cards after the spell's initial casting cost has been paid. Channel cards increase the spell's effect according to the spell's description.

Divine Spell Access

Clerics gain access to the complete Divine spell list appropriate to their level.

Tier 1 at level 1, Tier 2 at level 3, and Tier 3 at level 5.

Tier 4 and Tier 5 Spells are gained via Feats.

IV

Knowledge Domain

Clerics of the Knowledge Domain seek understanding above all else. Their divine gifts reveal secrets, expose weaknesses, and allow them to understand creatures, artifacts, and forgotten places.

Play Style: Investigation, information gathering, magical knowledge, and exploiting enemy weaknesses.

Knowledge Domain Features

Divine Scholar

Gain proficiency in Arcana, History, or Investigation.

Revelation

When you successfully use Arcane Insight, you may ask one additional question about the target.

Shared Knowledge

When you reveal an enemy's weakness, choose one ally who can immediately draw one card.

Read the Enemy

Once per scene, after observing an enemy, you may ask the GM which of its known attacks or abilities is most dangerous.

Forbidden Lore

Gain proficiency in Religion/Occult if you do not already have it. When investigating supernatural phenomena, you may treat a 2 as a 3 for the purpose of determining the result of the check.

Divine Memory

Once per scene, when making an Arcana, History, or Investigation check, you may discard a card to draw one additional card before resolving the check.

V

War Domain

Clerics of the War Domain channel divine power through strength, discipline, and righteous battle. They stand among their allies rather than behind them.

Play Style: Front-line combat, weapon attacks, protection, battlefield leadership, and aggressive use of divine power.

War Domain Features

War Priest

Gain proficiency with one additional weapon set.

Divine Strike

Once per turn when you hit with a Heart weapon, you may burn an additional Heart to deal +1 damage.

Battle Prayer

Before making a weapon attack, you may discard a Heart to gain +1 to the attack.

Hold the Line

When an adjacent ally is hit, you may burn a Heart as a reaction to reduce the damage they take by 1 HP.

Righteous Fury

When you reduce an enemy to 0 HP, you may immediately draw one card.

War Cry

When using Rallying Cry, you may choose to give one affected ally +1 to their next attack instead of allowing them to draw a card.
