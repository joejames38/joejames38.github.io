Wizard

Arcane scholars who manipulate magic through study, preparation, and mastery of the spellbook.

Primary Attribute Cunning
Resonant Suit Diamonds
Armor None
Weapons None
Spell Tradition Arcane

Wizards manipulate the fundamental forces of magic through knowledge and careful preparation. Their spellbooks allow them to accumulate a broad collection of spells and prepare for different challenges.

Wizard Identity

Wizards are flexible arcane spellcasters. Their greatest strength is not a single powerful ability, but the breadth of magical tools they can collect and bring into an adventure.

I

Starting Proficiencies

  • Weapons: None
  • Armor: None
  • Skills: Choose any 2 skills
  • Magic: Arcane spellcasting

A Wizard without armor or weapon proficiency is expected to rely on positioning, defensive magic, allies, and clever use of their spellbook.

Learning Spells

A Wizard learns one additional spell whenever they gain a level, provided the spell is of a tier they currently have access to.

III

The Wizard's Spellbook

A Wizard's spellbook is the foundation of their magical practice. Spells recorded in the spellbook can be studied, prepared, and used by the Wizard.

Starting Spellbook

At level 1, a Wizard begins with:

  • 3 Tier 0 Arcane spells
  • 3 Tier 1 Arcane spells
Learning a Spell

Whenever a Wizard gains a level, they learn one Arcane spell of a tier they currently have access to.

The Wizard chooses the spell and records it permanently in their spellbook.

Copying from a Scroll

A Wizard may copy a spell from a magical scroll into their spellbook.

The process costs 50 gold per spell tier. Tier 0 spells have no copying cost.

The scroll is consumed during the copying process.

  • Tier 0: Free
  • Tier 1: 50 gold
  • Tier 2: 100 gold
  • Tier 3: 150 gold
  • Tier 4: 200 gold
  • Tier 5: 250 gold
Copying from Another Wizard

A Wizard may copy a spell from another Wizard's spellbook.

The same 50 gold per spell tier cost applies.

Unlike copying from a scroll, copying from another spellbook does not consume or damage the original spellbook.

Spellbook Expansion

There is no stated maximum number of spells that can be recorded in a Wizard's spellbook. A Wizard who invests time and money into research can therefore become an extremely versatile spellcaster.

IV

Arcane Spellcasting

Wizards use Diamonds as their resonant suit for Arcane magic.

When casting a spell during combat, burn the required number of cards. The first card contributes to the spell's magic attack check.

Additional cards used to pay the casting cost do not normally contribute to the attack check. However, each additional card matching the spell's resonant suit provides +1 to the check.

Spells with the Channel property may burn additional resonant cards after the initial casting cost has been paid. Channeling increases the spell's effect according to its description.

Wizard Spell Access

Wizards gain access to:

  • Tier 0 spells at level 1
  • Tier 1 spells at level 1
  • Tier 2 spells at level 3
  • Tier 3 spells at level 5

Higher-tier spells may be added through future class features, feats, or campaign progression.

V

Evoker

Evokers specialize in direct magical force. They manipulate elemental energy, increase spell damage, and reshape spells to suit the battlefield.

Play Style: High-impact spells, damage enhancement, elemental manipulation, and battlefield control.

Evoker Features

Elemental Scholar

When you learn a damaging Arcane spell, you may choose its damage type from the types available to that spell.

Empowered Spell

Once per turn when an Arcane spell deals damage, you may burn an additional Diamond to deal +1 damage.

Elemental Substitution

When casting a damaging spell, you may change its damage type to another appropriate elemental type.

Overchannel

Once per scene, burn an additional Diamond when casting a damaging spell to increase its damage by +2.

Careful Evocation

When one of your area spells would damage an ally, you may exclude one ally from the effect.

Arcane Momentum

When you reduce an enemy to 0 HP with an Arcane spell, draw one card.

VI

Illusionist

Illusionists manipulate perception rather than relying solely on raw magical force. Their magic creates distractions, false targets, altered appearances, and opportunities for their allies.

Play Style: Flexible spell management, deception, distraction, battlefield manipulation, and creative problem solving.

Illusionist Features

Flexible Illusion

Once per scene, you may describe a minor alteration to an illusion spell without changing its mechanical effect.

Phantom Target

When you successfully create an illusion that distracts an enemy, that enemy suffers a penalty to its next attack against a target other than the illusion.

False Opening

When an enemy is distracted by one of your illusions, the next ally to attack that enemy may draw one additional card for the attack check.

Illusory Escape

When targeted by an attack, burn a card to create a convincing illusion of yourself somewhere nearby. The attack misses if the GM determines the illusion could reasonably fool the attacker.

Borrowed Appearance

You may use illusion magic to temporarily mimic the appearance, clothing, or voice of another creature.

Spell Mirage

Once per scene, when you cast an Arcane spell, you may create a harmless visual duplicate of the spell's effect elsewhere within Near range. The duplicate is purely illusory but may distract creatures.

VII

Wizard Feats

These feats support continued development of the Wizard after reaching level 5.

Spellbook Adept

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Wizard

Reduce the gold cost of copying spells by 25%.

Expanded Spellbook

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Wizard

Learn two additional Arcane spells that you currently have access to.

Evocation Mastery

Cost: 3 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Evoker

Once per turn, when you channel a damaging spell, the first additional Channel card deals +2 damage instead of +1.

Master of Illusions

Cost: 3 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Illusionist

Once per scene, cast a Tier 1 illusion spell without burning a card.

Ritualist

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Wizard

When casting a non-combat spell outside combat, reduce its effective casting cost by 1 card, to a minimum of 0.

Arcane Reservoir

Cost: 3 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Wizard

Once per short rest, when you burn the last card in your hand to cast a spell, immediately draw one card.

VIII

Playtest Notes

The Wizard's spellbook is intended to be one of the major long-term progression systems for the class. The absence of a hard spellbook limit allows the Wizard to become increasingly versatile through exploration, treasure, research, and interaction with other spellcasters.

The cost of copying spells should be monitored during playtesting. The current value is 50 gold per spell tier, with Tier 0 spells being free.

Evoker and Illusionist are intentionally designed to encourage very different uses of the same spell library. Evokers should feel mechanically powerful and direct, while Illusionists should have more freedom to solve encounters creatively.
