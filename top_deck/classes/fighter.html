Fighter

Masters of weapons and armor who turn every card into an opportunity for decisive action.

Primary Attribute Might
Resonant Suit Spades
Armor All Armor and Shields
Weapons Spade, Heart, and Club Weapons
Role Warrior / Defender / Striker

Fighters are the most broadly trained combatants in Realm & Rune. They can use every weapon, every armor type, and shields, allowing them to adapt to almost any battlefield.

Fighter Identity

Fighters are defined by reliability, durability, and tactical flexibility. Their large selection of equipment allows them to change their approach without changing their fundamental class.

I

Starting Proficiencies

  • Weapons: All weapons
  • Armor: Light, Medium, and Heavy Armor
  • Shields: Shields
  • Skills: Choose any 2 skills

Fighters are trained to operate effectively with almost any conventional weapon or armor.

III

Fighter Features

Second Wind

Once per short rest, discard one card to recover 1 HP.

Second Wind represents the Fighter's ability to push through exhaustion and continue fighting.

Action Surge

Once per short rest, discard any face card and draw cards until you reach your current hand size.

Action Surge does not provide an additional action by itself. Instead, it refreshes the Fighter's available cards, allowing them to continue taking actions during their turn.

Weapon Mastery

At level 1, the Fighter gains a Weapon Mastery feat. Weapon Mastery feats represent specialized training with a particular weapon category.

A weapon's suit affinity may provide an additional damage effect or tactical property when the matching card is played.

Fighter and Card Economy

Fighters have more opportunities than most classes to convert cards into combat actions. Weaponmaster features further emphasize this relationship by allowing the Fighter to make deliberate choices about when to spend, retain, or manipulate cards.

IV

Weaponmaster

Weaponmasters are students of the battlefield who believe every weapon has a purpose. Rather than committing to a single fighting style, they switch weapons and techniques to exploit the situation in front of them.

Play Style: Weapon versatility, card manipulation, melee flexibility, tactical reactions, and exploiting weapon suit affinities.

Weaponmaster Features

Versatile Training

When you equip a weapon, you may treat one weapon property associated with that weapon's suit as a Weaponmaster technique.

Weapon Swap

Once per turn, you may equip or stow a weapon as part of your Move action.

This allows you to change weapons without spending your Action.

Perfect Match

When you play a card matching your weapon's suit affinity, you may treat the attack as having its weapon mastery effect even if the weapon normally requires a different condition.

Combat Rhythm

If you successfully hit with two different weapon categories during the same scene, draw one card.

Riposte

When an enemy misses you with a melee attack, you may burn a matching weapon-suit card to make a basic melee attack against that enemy.

Heavy Blow

When wielding a weapon associated with Spades, you may burn an additional Spade after a successful Might Strike to deal +1 damage.

Shield Master

When using a shield, your Shield Bash deals +1 damage.

Weapon Specialist

Choose one weapon category. Once per turn, when using that category, you may treat a number card as a face card for determining its weapon effect.

V

Bowmaster

Bowmasters specialize in ranged combat, battlefield awareness, and supporting their allies from a distance. They use positioning and careful observation rather than relying exclusively on raw damage.

Play Style: Ranged attacks, stealth, scouting, tactical support, and controlling the battlefield from a distance.

Bowmaster Features

Bow Training

Gain proficiency with bows. When making a Ranged Strike with a bow, you may use your Dexterity modifier.

Called Shot

Before making a ranged attack, declare a specific target location or object. On a successful hit, you may apply a minor tactical effect appropriate to the target.

Examples include forcing an enemy to drop an object, pinning clothing to a surface, breaking a fragile object, or cutting a rope.

Covering Fire

Play a ranged attack card without dealing damage. Until the beginning of your next turn, an ally moving through the affected area gains +1 AC against ranged attacks.

Hunter's Mark

Play a card to mark one enemy you can see. The next ally to successfully attack that enemy deals +1 damage.

Silent Archer

Gain advantage on Stealth checks made while carrying or using a bow.

Hidden Shot

When you successfully attack from Hide with a bow, you may immediately attempt another Stealth check to remain hidden.

Warning Shot

Fire a non-damaging shot to create a distraction. One creature within Near range must succeed on an appropriate check or become distracted until the beginning of your next turn.

Guiding Arrow

When you hit an enemy with a bow, choose one ally. That ally gains +1 to their next attack against the same enemy.

VI

Fighter Feats

These feats provide additional development for Fighters after reaching level 5.

Weapon Specialist

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Fighter

Gain proficiency with one weapon category and gain its associated Weapon Mastery effect.

Martial Adept

Cost: 3 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Fighter

Learn two additional Weapon Mastery feats.

Battle Hardened

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Fighter

Once per scene, when reduced to 0 HP, discard a card to remain at 1 HP.

Shield Expert

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Shield proficiency

When using Defend with a shield, increase the AC bonus provided by the discarded card by +1.

Tactical Draw

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Fighter

Once per short rest, after successfully completing an attack, draw one card.

Longshot

Cost: 2 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Bowmaster

Your bow attacks can treat Far range as Near range for the purpose of attack penalties or other range-based effects.

Tactical Commander

Cost: 3 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Fighter

Once per scene, when you successfully hit an enemy, choose an ally. That ally may immediately draw one card.

Master of Arms

Cost: 3 Feat Points

Prerequisite: Weaponmaster

Once per turn, when you switch weapons, the next attack you make with the new weapon gains +1 to its attack check.
