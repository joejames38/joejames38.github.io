Fighter

Masters of weapons and armor who turn every card into an opportunity for decisive action.

Resonant Suit Spades

Role Warrior / Defender / Striker

Fighters are the most broadly trained combatants in Realm & Rune. They can use every weapon, every armor type, and shields, allowing them to adapt to almost any battlefield.

Fighter Identity

Fighters are defined by reliability, durability, and tactical flexibility. Their large selection of equipment allows them to change their approach without changing their fundamental class.

I

Starting Proficiencies

  • Weapons: All weapons
  • Armor: Light, Medium, and Heavy Armor
  • Shields: Shields
  • Skills: Choose any 2 skills

Fighters are trained to operate effectively with almost any conventional weapon or armor.

III

Fighter Features

Second Wind

Burn a 2 and recover 2 hp.

Second Wind represents the Fighter's ability to push through exhaustion and continue fighting.

Action Surge

Once per short rest, discard any face card and draw cards until you reach your current hand size.

Action Surge does not provide an additional action by itself. Instead, it refreshes the Fighter's available cards, allowing them to continue taking actions during their turn.

Weapon Mastery

At level 1, the Fighter gains a Weapon Mastery feat. Weapon Mastery feats represent specialized training with a particular weapon category.

A weapon's suit affinity may provide an additional damage effect or tactical property when the matching card is played.

Fighter and Card Economy

Fighters have more opportunities than most classes to convert cards into combat actions. Weaponmaster features further emphasize this relationship by allowing the Fighter to make deliberate choices about when to spend, retain, or manipulate cards.

IV

Weaponmaster

Weaponmasters are students of the battlefield who believe every weapon has a purpose. Rather than committing to a single fighting style, they switch weapons and techniques to exploit the situation in front of them.

Play Style: Weapon versatility, card manipulation, melee flexibility, tactical reactions, and exploiting weapon suit affinities.

Weaponmaster Features

Weapon Swap

Once per turn, you may equip or stow a weapon as part of your Move action.

This does not include swapping out a shield.

This allows you to change weapons without spending your Action.

V

Bowmaster

Bowmasters specialize in ranged combat, battlefield awareness, and supporting their allies from a distance. They use positioning and careful observation rather than relying exclusively on raw damage.

Play Style: Ranged attacks, stealth, scouting, tactical support, and controlling the battlefield from a distance.

Bowmaster Features

Bow Training

Gain proficiency with bows. When making a Ranged Strike with a bow, you may use your Dexterity or Might modifier.
