I

Backgrounds

Your background describes what your character did before becoming an adventurer. It provides practical skills, experience, and a small advantage that remains useful throughout the game.

Every Background Provides:
  • Choose 2 of 4 listed skill proficiencies.
  • Gain 1 minor feat or special ability.

Backgrounds do not modify Core Attributes. Your background should help define what your character knows and how they approach the world without determining their class.

II

Background Skill Reference

The following skills are available when selecting background proficiencies.

Attribute Skills
Might — ♠ Athletics, Intimidation, Survival
Spirit — ♥ Insight, Persuasion, Deception, Religion/Occult, Performance
Dexterity — ♣ Acrobatics, Thievery, Animal Handling, Stealth
Cunning — ♦ Perception, Arcana, Investigation, Medicine, History
III

Acolyte

You spent your formative years serving a temple, religious order, or spiritual community. You understand rituals, doctrine, and the responsibilities of those who serve something greater than themselves.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose 2: Religion/Occult, Insight, Medicine, or Performance.

Acolyte's Faith: Learn one Tier 1 Divine Spell.
IV

Artisan

You learned a trade through years of practice. You know how things are made, how tools are used, and how to judge the quality of someone's work.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose 2: Insight, History, Investigation, or Perception.

Tradecraft: You can identify the purpose, approximate quality, and common construction of ordinary manufactured objects. When repairing or crafting a simple object, you may complete work that would normally require basic tools without needing a separate skill check.
V

Criminal

You survived by breaking laws, avoiding authorities, running scams, stealing what you needed, or working alongside people who did.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose 2: Thievery, Deception, Stealth, or Investigation.

Criminal Contacts: You know how to locate the local underworld in a settlement. When looking for illegal goods, information, or contacts, you can usually identify where to begin without a skill check.
VI

Hunter

You lived by tracking animals, traveling through the wilderness, and reading the signs left behind by living creatures.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose 2: Animal Handling, Survival, Perception, or Stealth.

Hunter's Eye: When examining tracks, signs of passage, or a natural trail, you can determine the general direction and approximate age of the trail without making a skill check.
VII

Noble

You were raised among wealth, influence, or political power. You understand etiquette, social expectations, and the invisible rules that govern powerful people.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose 2: Persuasion, Deception, History, or Insight.

Noble Confidence: Gain advantage on social skill checks made with Nobles.
VIII

Scholar

You spent years studying books, ancient records, magical theory, or other specialized knowledge. You approach problems through research and careful reasoning.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose 2: Arcana, History, Investigation, or Medicine.

Researcher: When you have access to a library, archive, or substantial collection of written records, you can identify useful sources of information without making a skill check.
IX

Soldier

You served in a military, militia, mercenary company, city watch, or other organized fighting force.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose 2: Athletics, Intimidation, Survival, or Perception.

Weapon Training: Gain proficiency in one weapon set of your choice.
X

Wanderer

You spent your life traveling from place to place. Roads, wilderness, settlements, and strangers became familiar parts of your life.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose 2: Perception, Survival, Stealth, or Persuasion.

Roadwise: You can usually identify the safest common route through familiar terrain and can find basic food, water, and shelter while traveling through a populated region.
XI

Background Design

Backgrounds are intended to provide a small but meaningful mechanical identity without competing with ancestry or class.

Background Formula

Choose 2 of 4 Skills + 1 Minor Feat or Ability

Background abilities should generally be useful outside of the character's primary class abilities. They may provide social advantages, exploration benefits, knowledge, minor equipment benefits, or situational mechanical advantages.

Backgrounds should not normally increase Core Attributes, increase Hand Size, or provide major combat abilities. Those areas belong primarily to ancestry, class, and feats.
