Realm & Rune
Magic Rules
I

Magic

Magic in Realm & Rune is powered by the same cards used for combat and skill checks. Spellcasting therefore creates a meaningful choice between spending cards now and preserving them for later.

Unlike ordinary actions, cards used to cast spells are Burned. They are removed from the active card cycle and do not return until the character completes a Long Rest.

The Four Resonances

Each magical tradition is associated with one suit. A spell's Resonant Suit determines which cards empower the spell.

II

Reading a Spell

Every spell in the Realm & Rune spell library contains the following information.

Field Meaning
Name The spell's name.
Tier The spell's power level and casting cost.
Resonance The suit associated with the spell.
Range Close, Near, or Far.
Target Self, Enemy, Ally, or another listed target.
Duration How long the spell's effect persists.
Classes Classes that can learn or cast the spell.
Channel Indicates whether additional Resonant cards can increase the spell's damage.
Description The spell's mechanical and narrative effect.
