Realm & Rune
Magic Rules
I

Magic

Magic in Realm & Rune is powered by the same cards used for combat and skill checks. Spellcasting therefore creates a meaningful choice between spending cards now and preserving them for later.

Unlike ordinary actions, cards used to cast spells are Burned. They are removed from the active card cycle and do not return until the character completes a Long Rest.

The Four Resonances

Each magical tradition is associated with one suit. A spell's Resonant Suit determines which cards empower the spell.

II

The Four Resonances

Tradition Resonant Suit Theme
Arcane ♦ Diamonds Knowledge, elemental forces, manipulation of reality
Divine ♥ Hearts Faith, healing, protection, divine power
Primal ♣ Clubs Nature, beasts, elements, physical transformation
Occult ♠ Spades Secrets, curses, spirits, forbidden forces
III

Spell Tiers

Spells are divided into six tiers, from Tier 0 through Tier 5. Higher-tier spells require more cards and produce more powerful effects.

Tier Base Cards Burned Power
Tier 0 0 outside combat / 1 in combat Simple magical effects and cantrips
Tier 1 1 Basic combat and utility magic
Tier 2 2 Strong combat and meaningful utility
Tier 3 3 Major magical effects
Tier 4 4 Powerful battlefield and narrative magic
Tier 5 5 Exceptional magic capable of changing an encounter
IV

Casting Spells in Combat

Casting a spell in combat requires the caster to Burn the number of cards specified by the spell's Tier.

Only the first card Burned for the spell contributes its numerical value to the spell's magic attack.

Magic Attack

First Card Value + Caster's Appropriate Attribute + Resonant Suit Bonuses

Every additional card in the spell's casting cost is normally used only to pay the spell's resource cost.

Resonant Cards

Each card after the first that matches the spell's Resonant Suit adds +1 to the spell's attack check.

Cards that do not match the spell's Resonant Suit still count toward the casting cost, but provide no additional attack bonus.

Example: Tier 3 Arcane Spell

A Wizard casts a Tier 3 Arcane spell and burns:

9♦, 4♦, 2♣

The first card, 9♦, provides a +9 card value. The second card, 4♦, resonates with Arcane magic and provides +1 to the attack. The 2♣ pays the casting cost but provides no bonus.

The final magic attack therefore receives: 9 + Cunning modifier + 1.

V

Casting Magic Outside Combat

Magic is easier to control outside the pressure of combat. When casting a spell outside combat, the caster may search their Deck for the cards required to pay the spell's cost.

Every card selected must match the spell's Resonant Suit.

The selected cards are then Burned.

Example

A Wizard needs to illuminate a dark ruin using a Tier 1 Arcane spell. Because the spell is being cast outside combat, the Wizard may search their Deck for one Diamond and Burn it.

This rule allows characters to use appropriate utility magic without relying entirely on random card draws, while still making magic consume a finite resource.

VI

Tier 0 — Cantrips

Tier 0 spells are simple magical effects. They do not deal damage and are intended to provide utility, flavor, and creative problem-solving.

Outside combat, Tier 0 spells may be cast freely and require no cards.

In combat, casting a Tier 0 spell requires Burning one card. Any suit may be used.

Tier 0 Design Principle

Cantrips should solve small problems without replacing equipment, skills, or higher-tier spells.

VII

Channel

Some spells possess the Channel property. Channeling allows a caster to Burn additional Resonant Suit cards after paying the spell's initial casting cost.

Channeling is applied after the spell's initial cost has been paid and the spell has been resolved.

Each additional Resonant Suit card Burned through Channel increases the spell's damage by +1.

Channel Damage

Base Spell Damage \+ 1 damage per additional Resonant card
Example: Firebolt

Firebolt is a Tier 1 Arcane spell and normally deals 1 HP on a successful hit.

The Wizard pays the normal casting cost by Burning one card. After the spell is cast, the Wizard may Channel by Burning additional Diamonds.

Burning two additional Diamonds increases Firebolt's damage from 1 HP to 3 HP.

Channeling is optional. A caster never has to spend additional cards after paying the spell's normal cost.

VIII

Magic Attacks

A spell requiring a magic attack uses the caster's appropriate magical attribute.

Tradition Primary Attribute
Arcane Cunning
Divine Spirit
Primal Might or Dexterity, depending on the spell
Occult Cunning

Unless a spell specifically states otherwise, a successful magic attack deals the spell's listed effect.

IX

Reading a Spell

Every spell in the Realm & Rune spell library contains the following information.

Field Meaning
Name The spell's name.
Tier The spell's power level and casting cost.
Resonance The suit associated with the spell.
Range Close, Near, or Far.
Target Self, Enemy, Ally, or another listed target.
Duration How long the spell's effect persists.
Classes Classes that can learn or cast the spell.
Channel Indicates whether additional Resonant cards can increase the spell's damage.
Description The spell's mechanical and narrative effect.

XII

Magic Playtest Questions

  • Does Burning multiple cards make high-tier spells appropriately rare?
  • Does the first-card-only attack value produce an appropriate hit rate?
  • Are Resonant Suit bonuses strong enough to encourage thematic card management?
  • Is Channel powerful enough to be exciting without making low-tier spells outperform higher-tier spells?
  • Does searching the Deck for Resonant cards outside combat create useful utility without making resource management irrelevant?
  • Are Tier 0 cantrips useful without invalidating mundane equipment or skills?
  • How often can characters realistically cast Tier 3–5 spells during a typical adventure?
  • Does the Burn Pile create meaningful long-term resource pressure?
