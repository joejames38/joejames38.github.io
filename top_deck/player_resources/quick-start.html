Realm & Rune
A Card-Driven Fantasy Roleplaying Game

Quick Start

Realm & Rune is a heroic fantasy tabletop roleplaying game using standard playing cards instead of dice.

The Core Idea: Your cards are both your dice and your action economy. Strong cards give you better opportunities, but spending them means they will not be immediately available later.

What You Need

  • One standard 52-card deck per player.
  • A character sheet.
  • Pencils or another writing tool.
  • A way to track HP and temporary effects.

Build Your Character

  1. Choose an Ancestry.
  2. Choose two Ancestry Feats.
  3. Choose a Background.
  4. Apply Background skill proficiencies.
  5. Choose a Core Class.
  6. Apply Level 1 class features.
  7. Determine your Core Attributes.
  8. Choose starting equipment.

Starting Attributes

All Attributes begin at -1. You have 6 points to distribute among them, with no Attribute exceeding +2.

Card Zones

Deck

Your active draw pile.

Hand

Cards available to play.

Shuffle Pile

Played cards normally go here.

Burn Pile

Permanently unavailable until a Long Rest.

When the Deck is empty, shuffle the Shuffle Pile into a new Deck. Burned cards remain unavailable.

Checks & Skills

When something is uncertain and failure matters, the GM calls for a check.

Check = Card Value + Attribute Modifier

If the total equals or exceeds the Difficulty, you succeed. Meets it, Beats it.

Advantage & Disadvantage

Situation Procedure
Normal Play one card.
Advantage Draw 2; use the more favorable result.
Disadvantage Draw 2; use the less favorable result.

Skill Training

Training Procedure
Untrained Disadvantage. Draw 2 and use the lower card.
Proficient Draw and play 1 card.
Expertise Advantage. Draw 2 and use the higher card.

Combat

Initiative

Initiative = Top Card + Dexterity

Players win ties against enemies. Place the Initiative card in the Shuffle Pile.

If the Initiative card is a face card, you may Burn it to gain Acceleration and draw one additional card at the beginning of your first turn.

Your Turn

  1. Draw one card.
  2. Take one Move.
  3. Use Actions by playing cards.
  4. Use a Reaction if its trigger occurs.
  5. Return to maximum Hand Size at the end of your turn.
Resource Cost
Move No card.
Action Normally 1 card.
Reaction Normally 1 card; one per turn.
Free Activity Talking or dropping an item.

Attacks & Defense

Attack = Card Value + Relevant Attribute

If the Attack Total equals or exceeds the target's AC, the attack hits.

Basic Attacks

Might Strike

Play 1 card. Make a Might attack with a proficient weapon. Hit: 1 HP damage.

Ranged Strike

Play 1 card. Make a ranged attack with a proficient weapon. Hit: 1 HP damage.

Defend

Play 1 card. Add its value to AC until your next turn.

Parry

Reaction. Play 1 card to negate 1 HP of physical damage. A face card may negate all damage from one physical source.

Weapons

Weapons are divided into broad suit categories. You must have proficiency with a weapon group to use its weapons.

Suit Weapon Theme Examples
♠ Spades Melee / Martial Swords
♥ Hearts Support / Ceremonial Maces
♣ Clubs Agile / Precision Daggers, short swords
♦ Diamonds Technical / Magical Wands
Ranged Specialized Longbows, crossbows, pistols
Weapon Resonance: When proficient with a suit's weapons, you gain resonance with cards of that suit, dealing +1 damage.
Weapon Mastery: If two character-creation sources grant proficiency with the same weapon type, gain the associated Weapon Mastery.

Spell Costs

Tier Cost
0 Free outside combat; Burn any card in combat.
1 1 resonant card
2 2 resonant cards
3 3 resonant cards
4 4 resonant cards
5 5 resonant cards

The first card Burned provides its value for the magic attack. Each additional resonant card adds +1 to the magic attack.

Magic Surge: A resonant face card counts as 2 cards toward the casting cost. An off-suit face card counts as 1.

Channel: A spell with Channel may Burn additional cards to strengthen its effect.

Concentration

Some spells require constant focus to maintain their effect.

One Concentration Spell: A character may have only one concentration spell active at a time.

If a player takes damage or another effect threatens concentration, the player loses concentration unless they Burn the top card of their deck.

Food & Gobble

When a player has an Ace in their hand, they may use the Gobble action.

Gobble

Special Action — Burn an Ace.

Burn an Ace and consume a food, drink, potion, or other consumable. Apply its effect.

Persistent Food: Only one persistent food effect may be active at a time. A new persistent food replaces the previous one.
Item Effect
Bread Recover 1 HP.
Health Potion Recover 3 HP.
Protein Bar +1 Might until Long Rest.
Fizzy Drink +1 Dexterity until Long Rest.
Arcane Tea +1 Cunning until Long Rest.
Comfort Stew +1 Spirit until Long Rest.

HP & Defeat

When a player reaches 0 HP, they fall unconscious and fall to the ground.

An unconscious character cannot fight, move, or take normal actions.

Whenever an unconscious character gains HP from any source, they immediately regain consciousness and may act normally.

Helping an Unconscious Character: Only liquid drinks and potions may be given to another player to assist in recovering their HP.
Party Defeat: If the entire party is unconscious, the DM determines the fate of the party.

Rest & Advancement

Short Rest

  • Shuffle the Shuffle Pile into the Deck.
  • Recover abilities that specify Short Rest.
  • Take a Gobble action if desired.
  • Cast spells using out-of-combat casting rules.

Long Rest

  • Recover all HP.
  • Return Burn and Shuffle Piles to the Deck.
  • Persistent food effects expire.
