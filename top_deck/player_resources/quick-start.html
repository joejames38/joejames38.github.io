Realm & Rune

Quick Start Guide — Player Playtest Edition

1. Make Your Character

  1. Choose an Ancestry and two Ancestry Feats.
  2. Choose a Background and gain its two skill proficiencies and minor ability.
  3. Choose a Class and apply Level 1 features.
  4. Set Attributes: Might, Spirit, Dexterity, Cunning.
  5. Choose equipment and prepare your starting deck.
Starting Attributes Standard Array: −1, 0, +1, +2. Allocation: Start all at −1 and distribute 6 points; maximum +2 in any attribute.

2. Your Cards

DeckEach player has a personal deck. Normally 52 cards; Jokers are optional.
HandYour available cards. At the start of a scene, draw to your maximum hand size. At the start of your turn, draw 1.
Shuffle PileOrdinary actions, attacks, reactions, and skill checks go here.
Burn PileCards used by effects that say Burn leave normal circulation until recovered by resting.

When your deck is empty, shuffle the Shuffle Pile into a new deck. Burned cards remain unavailable.

3. Checks

Check Total = Card Value + Attribute Modifier

The GM sets the Difficulty. Meets it, beats it: equal to or higher succeeds.

TrainingProcedure
UntrainedDisadvantage on the check.
ProficientPlay 1 card and add the skill's attribute modifier.
ExpertisePlay up to 2 cards. If two are played, the second must match the skill's suit affinity. Add both card values and the attribute modifier.
AdvantagePlay a card from your hand, draw and play the top card of your deck, then use the most favorable result.
DisadvantagePlay a card from your hand, draw and play the top card of your deck, then use the least favorable result.

On your turn: Draw 1 card → take 1 Move (no card required) → play cards for Actions as desired. You may spend your whole hand if you want to.

Remember Your cards are both your randomizer and your resources. A powerful card spent now is a card you may not have later. Decide when to push your luck, when to conserve, and when to burn a card for something extraordinary.

Quick Start Guide — Combat, Magic & Survival

5. Combat

Flip for Initiative: play the top card of your deck and add Dexterity.

Initiative = Card Value + Dexterity

Highest acts first. Players win ties against enemies. If your Initiative card is a face card, you may Burn it for Acceleration: draw 1 additional card at the beginning of your first turn.

Armor & Attacks

Attack Total = Card Value + Relevant Attribute Modifier

If the Attack Total equals or exceeds AC, the attack hits.

ArmorAC

Might Strike — Action

Play 1 card. Might attack. Hit: 1 HP. Matching weapon suit: 2 HP.

Ranged Strike — Action

Play 1 card. Dexterity attack. Hit: 1 HP. Matching weapon suit: 2 HP.

Defend — Action

Play 1 card. Add its value to AC until your next turn.

Dodge — Reaction

Play 1 card when hit to negate 1 HP. Burn a face card to negate all damage from one source.

Shield Bash — Action

Play a Spade. Hit: 1 HP and push or knock prone. Burn a face-card Spade to do both.

Rallying Cry — Action

Play a Heart. One ally draws 1. Burn a face-card Heart: two allies each draw 1.

Arcane Insight — Action

Play a Diamond. Reveal useful information about an enemy. Burn a face-card Diamond to affect two.

Backstab

Use the Rogue ability and follow the current Rogue class page.

6. Magic

DomainSuitTheme
Arcane♦Elements, force, magical manipulation.
Divine♥Healing, protection, blessings, judgment.
Primal♣Nature, beasts, storms, transformation.
Occult♠Secrets, spirits, curses, shadows.
Tier 0Free outside combat. In combat, Burn any card to cast it.
Tiers 1–5Burn 1–5 cards of the spell's Resonant Suit.
Magic SurgeA resonant face card counts as 2 cards toward the spell cost. An off-suit face card counts as 1.
Combat CastingThe first card Burned supplies its value to the magic attack. Each additional Resonant Suit card adds +1 to the attack.
Outside CombatYou may search your deck for the Resonant Suit cards needed to pay the spell cost, then Burn them.
ChannelAfter paying the normal cost, some spells let you Burn additional cards to strengthen their effect. Follow the spell's Channel text.

7. Gobble, HP & Recovery

Gobble — Special ActionBurn an Ace and consume a food, drink, potion, or other consumable. Apply its effect. Only one persistent food effect may be active at once.
0 HPAt 0 HP, you fall unconscious and to the ground. You cannot fight or take normal actions. Gain HP from any source and you regain consciousness.
Helping an Unconscious AllyOnly liquid drinks and potions may be given to another player to assist in recovering HP. If the entire party is unconscious, the GM determines the party's fate.
