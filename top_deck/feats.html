I

Feats

Feats represent abilities learned through experience, training, experimentation, and adventure. Unlike class features, feats can be acquired independently and allow characters to develop in unusual directions.

Feat Points: The standard starting cost for a feat is 2 Feat Points.

Characters may acquire additional Feat Points as the campaign progresses. The GM may award Feat Points for milestones, discoveries, major accomplishments, or other campaign achievements.

Feats are intentionally independent of level. Once a character has access to enough Feat Points and meets a feat's prerequisite, the feat may be purchased.

II

Feat Types

Feats are organized into broad categories to make the growing feat library easier to navigate.

  • Combat: Improve attacks, defense, weapons, or battlefield tactics.
  • Skill: Improve skill checks, proficiency, or expertise.
  • Magic: Expand spellcasting or magical capabilities.
  • Exploration: Improve travel, investigation, survival, and interaction with the environment.
  • Social: Improve persuasion, leadership, deception, and relationships.
  • Utility: Provide unusual abilities that do not fit neatly into another category.
