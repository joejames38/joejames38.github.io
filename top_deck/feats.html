Feat Rules
I

Feats

Feats represent abilities learned through experience, training, experimentation, and adventure. They provide characters with additional ways to customize their abilities after reaching the level cap.

Feat Points

The standard starting cost for a feat is 2 Feat Points.

More powerful feats may have higher costs.

II

Feat Types

Feats are divided into broad categories to make the growing feat library easier to navigate.

  • Combat — Weapons, attacks, defense, and battlefield abilities.
  • Skill — Skills, proficiency, and expertise.
  • Magic — Spellcasting and magical abilities.
  • Exploration — Travel, survival, investigation, and dungeon abilities.
  • Social — Persuasion, deception, leadership, and social interaction.
  • Utility — General-purpose abilities and unusual tricks.
