Character Development

Feats

Special talents, training, and abilities that distinguish one adventurer from another.

Using Feats

Feats represent specialized training, natural talent, or extraordinary experience. A feat grants your character benefits that go beyond the features provided by their class.

Origin Feats

Origin feats represent talents developed through a character's upbringing and background. Characters typically gain an Origin feat from their background during character creation.

General Feats

General feats represent additional training and specialization. Unless otherwise specified, these feats require character level 4 or higher. A character must meet all listed prerequisites to gain a feat.

Prerequisites

A character must meet a feat's prerequisites before selecting it. If a character later loses a prerequisite, they cannot benefit from that feat until the prerequisite is met again.
