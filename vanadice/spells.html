Spellcasting and Magic Points

Spells are magical abilities that characters learn through study, faith, or innate power. Each spell has a level that determines its Magic Point (MP) cost.

Spell Level and MP Cost

Cantrips are simple spells that require no MP. Leveled spells consume MP when cast.

Spell Lists

  • Arcane: Magic gained through knowledge, study, and manipulation of magical forces.
  • Divine: Magic drawn from faith, spiritual power, or a connection to divine forces.
A spell can appear on one or both spell lists.

Upcasting

Some spells become more powerful when cast using additional MP. Their descriptions identify the benefits gained when cast at a higher spell level.
