The Peoples of Vanadice

The world of Vanadice is home to many unique peoples, each with their own cultures, traditions, physical characteristics, and natural talents. Choose an ancestry that reflects your character's heritage and origins.

Choosing an Ancestry

Your ancestry represents your character's species and biological heritage. Each ancestry provides special traits that distinguish its members.

Ancestry determines your character's size, movement speed, and unique racial characteristics. Your ancestry does not determine your character's profession, personality, background, or class.

Ability Scores: Vanadice does not assign ability score increases through ancestry. Ability score improvements are determined through the character creation and advancement rules.
