Character Creation

Backgrounds

Your past shapes your skills, talents, and the abilities you bring to your adventures.

Choosing a Background

Your background represents your character's life and experiences before becoming an adventurer. It determines three ability score options, an Origin feat, two skill proficiencies, and one tool proficiency.

Ability Score Increases

Each background lists three eligible abilities. Choose one of the following options:

  • Option 1: Increase one listed ability by 2 and another listed ability by 1.
  • Option 2: Increase all three listed abilities by 1.

These increases cannot raise an ability score above 20. Apply these increases during character creation.

Background Benefits

  • Origin Feat: You gain the listed Origin feat.
  • Skill Proficiencies: You gain proficiency in both listed skills.
  • Tool Proficiency: You gain proficiency with the listed tool or tool category.
