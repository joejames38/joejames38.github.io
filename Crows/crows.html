Fortune or Death

The Dungeon Doesn't Care.

Crows: Fortune or Death is a survival-horror fantasy RPG built around dangerous dungeon crawls, wilderness travel, scarce resources, and the ever-present possibility that your adventurer simply won't come home.

Your character sheet won't save you. Your plan might. — The Unofficial Crows Survival Manual
Enter At Your Own Risk

Survival Is The Game

☠ Lethal Dungeons

Monsters, traps, hazards, and bad decisions can end an adventurer permanently. There is no promise that an encounter is fair.

🔥 Light Runs Out

Time matters. Dungeon Turns create pressure as torches burn, resources disappear, and the dungeon gets more dangerous.

🎒 Every Slot Matters

Your equipment determines what you can do. Limited inventory means every torch, ration, weapon, potion, and piece of treasure has a cost.

🩸 Wounds Matter

Damage isn't simply something to erase after combat. Injuries can reduce your ability to carry the very equipment you need to survive.

🗺 The Wilderness

Getting to the dungeon is part of the adventure. Navigation, travel pace, supplies, and encounters can turn the journey into its own survival story.

💰 Treasure Is Progress

The goal isn't simply to kill monsters. Bring treasure home, turn it into advancement, and decide how much you're willing to risk for it.

What You Leave Behind

The Town Remembers.

Your adventurers may not survive forever, but the town can. Treasure brought home can strengthen the community, improve its institutions, and give future adventurers a better chance of surviving the same darkness.

  • Bring back treasure. Your expedition should make the town richer.
  • Invest in the settlement. A stronger home means better opportunities for the next generation of adventurers.
  • Remember the dead. Fallen adventurers can leave something behind even when their journey ends.
  • Retirement is victory. Survive long enough and your adventurer can become part of the world instead of another name on a grave.
