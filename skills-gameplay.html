Game Basics

Game Master (GM): The GM guides the game, describes the world, controls NPCs, and adjudicates rules.

Roleplaying: Players assume the roles of their characters, making decisions and taking actions based on their character's perspective.

Dice: Different dice sizes are used:

  • d4 (4-sided)
  • d6 (6-sided)
  • d8 (8-sided)
  • d10 (10-sided)
  • d12 (12-sided)
  • d20 (20-sided)

Skill Checks: Roll a d20, add the relevant modifier, and compare to the DC.

Advantage/Disadvantage: Roll two d20s:

  • Advantage: Use the higher result.
  • Disadvantage: Use the lower result.

Combat

Initiative: Roll a d20 + Dexterity modifier to determine turn order.

Action Economy: On your turn, you can take:

  • 1 Action
  • 1 Bonus Action
  • 1 Reaction (outside of your turn)
  • Free Actions (simple tasks like speaking)

Ranged Weapons: Weapons with range may have disadvantage when shooting targets at close range.

Attack of Opportunity: A melee attack triggered when an enemy leaves your reach without disengaging.

Actions You Can Take:

  • Attack
  • Cast a Spell
  • Dash
  • Dodge
  • Disengage
  • Hide
  • Ready
  • Help

Game Progression

Episode-Based Sessions: Break your campaign into manageable episodes with a clear beginning, middle, and end.

Equipment Selection: Players should ensure they have the gear necessary for their goals (weapons, armor, tools, etc.).

Character Downtime: Use downtime for crafting, training, earning money, or pursuing personal goals.
